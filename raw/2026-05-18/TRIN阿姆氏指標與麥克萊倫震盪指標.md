## 來源1：taindicators - TRIN 交易者指數

Traders Index (TRIN) 交易者指數也叫作 Arms Index (因它是由Richard W. Arms 所發表)是一種領先的股市指標。它通過衡量主導市場的群體的樂觀程度，來顯示主要上漲和下跌趨勢何時將會反轉。市場頂部總會出現過度樂觀，市場底部總是悲觀過度。

### 計算方法

TRIN = (上漲的股票數量/下跌的股票數量) ÷ (上漲的股票的成交量/下跌的股票成交量)

然後通過一個簡單的平滑移動平均數計算：
- 短期建議 4 或 5 天均線
- 中期建議 20 或 21 天均線
- 長期建議 55 天均線

### 應用例子

TRIN是以1為中界線，而一般超買超賣指標，大部分都是以0或50為中界線，這是TRIN最大的特點。另一方面，適應不同市場及不同個股，其超買及超賣的位置也不相同。投資人必須經過長期的觀察之後，分別定立不同的標準。

當TRIN指標上升超越至其超賣天線區時，代表整體股市正進入超賣狀態。但是，必須等到TRIN曲線，重新向下跌破其超賣天線值時，才能確認市場底部完成。反之，當TRIN指標下降超越至其超買地線區時，代表整體股市正進入超買狀態。但是，必須等到TRIN曲線，重新向上突破其超買地線值時，才能確認市場頭部完成。

在正常發展的股票市場中，一個底部或一個頭部的形成，必然都須經過"轉彎"的過程。也就是說，市場的發展，由緩角度變成高角度之後，當其即將扭轉市場的方向時，必然會將高角度重新拉回緩角度。在角度緩減的過程中，上市公司的漲幅會逐漸變化，促使TRIN指標適時產生訊號。但是，有時候市場的發展並非正常化時，則TRIN指標的訊號，會出現落後的現象。例如：市場出現猛烈利空，行情不經過角度緩減的過程，直接向下銳挫。類似這種狀況的走勢，TRIN指標的參考價值將會降低。

## 來源2：MBA智庫百科 - 阿姆氏指標

阿姆氏指標（ARMS），英文Arms Index，1967年由小理查德·W·阿姆斯（Richard W·Arms，Jr）所創。一年之後，這個指標被陸續引用，衍生出許多不同名稱。例如以短線交易指標（Short-term Trading Index）命名，採用最後兩個單字的前兩個字母，稱之為TRIN。另外還有MKDS、STKS等名稱。該指標不僅經常用於短線交易，同時，也適用於長期投資。阿姆氏指標是一項非常重要的技術分析工具，其理論基礎——成交量變化是判斷未來行情走勢的主要依據。

ARMS屬於大勢指標，是一種判斷整體股市反轉點的分析工具。本指標是以上市公司漲跌家數的比例，結合漲跌股數成交量比率，對照兩者之間關係的變化，所求出的一條比率線。這一條比率線，可以偵測成交量的流向。當ARMS指標低於1時，代表大部分成交量流向上漲股。相反的，當指標高於1時，代表大部分成交量流向下跌股。換句話說，當指標低於1時，一般被視為多頭市場。指標高於1時，一般被視為空頭市場。

值得注意的是，本指標"天線"與"地線"的定義，和一般超買超賣指標的用法相反。TRIN上升超越超賣天線→超賣狀態；TRIN下降超越超買地線→超買狀態。

上證指數4天ARMS指標的超賣界限為2.4，21天ARMS指標的超賣界限為1.1。21天ARMS指標的超買界限為0.7。

劍法中所列數據，是小理查德·W·阿姆斯經過長期測試，所得出成果最佳的組合。但是，並非所有的市場都適用，不同的市場，必須加以調整修正。

當ARMS指標上升超越至其超賣天線區時，代表整體股市正進入超賣狀態。但是，必須等到ARMS曲線，重新向下跌破其超賣天線值時，才能確認市場底部完成。反之亦然。

ARMS指標較適合運用於穩定成熟的市場，過分投機的行情，必須配合成交量指標（VR）、情緒指標（BR）、人氣指標（AR）等等指標加以輔助。

## 來源3：StockCharts - Arms Index (TRIN)

The Arms Index, also known as the TRIN or Short-Term TRading INdex, is a breadth indicator developed by Richard W. Arms in 1967. The index is calculated by dividing the Advance-Decline Ratio by the Advance-Decline Volume Ratio.

### Calculation

TRIN = (Advances / Declines) / (Advancing Volume / Declining Volume)

- Advances: number of stocks that closed up on the day
- Declines: number of stocks that closed down on the day
- Advancing Volume: total volume of advancing stocks
- Declining Volume: total volume of declining stocks

### Interpretation

As a ratio of two indicators, the Arms Index reflects the relationship between the AD Ratio and the AD Volume Ratio. The TRIN is below one when the AD Volume Ratio is greater than the AD Ratio and above one when the AD Volume Ratio is less than the AD Ratio.

Low readings (below one) show relative strength in the AD Volume Ratio. High readings (above one) show relative weakness in the AD Volume Ratio.

In general, strong market advances are accompanied by relatively low TRIN readings because up-volume overwhelms down-volume. Strong market declines often push the Arms Index higher.

A strong up day in the market usually pushes the Arms Index lower, while a strong down day often pushes the Arms Index higher. The indicator appears to move "inverse" to the market.

### Chart Scaling

The Arms Index can be displayed with a semi-log scale or an arithmetic scale. Log scaling shows an equal distance for an equal percentage of movements, which evens out the fluctuations.

### Overbought/Oversold

Surges above three are deemed oversold, and dips below 0.50 are deemed overbought (for unmodified TRIN).

With a 10-day SMA, the range narrows: oversold above 1.20 and overbought below 0.80.

### Bottom Line

The Arms Index is a volatile breadth indicator that can generate overbought and oversold signals. Even though it generates plenty of signals, it is preferable to trade in the direction of the underlying trend.

## 來源4：StockCharts - McClellan Oscillator

Developed by Sherman and Marian McClellan, the McClellan Oscillator is a breadth indicator derived from Net Advances, which is the number of advancing issues less the number of declining issues.

### Calculation

Ratio-Adjusted Net Advances (RANA): (Advances - Declines)/(Advances + Declines)

McClellan Oscillator: 19-day EMA of RANA - 39-day EMA of RANA

The ratio adjustment makes it possible to compare McClellan Oscillator levels over a period of time, normalizing the data regardless of how many stocks are listed.

### Interpretation

Think of the McClellan Oscillator as the MACD for the AD Line. Just as MACD puts momentum into the price plot of a stock, the McClellan Oscillator puts momentum into the AD Line.

- Positive when 19-day EMA > 39-day EMA → advances gaining upper hand (bullish)
- Negative when 19-day EMA < 39-day EMA → declining issues dominating (bearish)

### Breadth Thrusts

A breadth thrust occurs when the McClellan Oscillator surges from deep negative readings to strong positive readings. Typically from below -50 to above +50 for a +100 point thrust. A breadth thrust signals a surge in bullish breadth that can lead to an extended advance. Most important lows are marked by a sharp surge in breadth.

A bullish breadth thrust is enhanced when preceded by a bullish divergence.

### Divergences

Bullish and bearish divergences in the McClellan Oscillator can foreshadow reversals in the underlying index. The key is to separate robust divergences from weak divergences:
1. A divergence should be confirmed with a strong supporting move
2. Divergences should be sharp — the difference between reaction highs or lows should be noticeable

### Bottom Line

The McClellan Oscillator measures the momentum of the AD Line or Net Advances. Like MACD, it is a rather volatile indicator that produces many potential signals. Signals should be confirmed or refuted with other technical indicators and chart analysis.

## 來源5：Finetic - 市場寬度

Arms Index（也稱為TRIN）是另一個市寬指標。該指標考慮了上升股票與下跌股票的比率，以及交易的股票數量。Arms Index 用於衡量市場情緒，它可以洞察股市是否超買或超賣。

McClellan Oscillator 和 McClellan Summation Index 是另外兩個常用的市場寬度指標。McClellan Oscillator著眼於股票指數的淨升幅和跌幅，而McClellan Summation Index則著眼於升幅和跌幅的累計總量。這兩個指標都可以用來衡量市場情緒，並可以洞察未來的市場方向。

市寬分析的危險：
- 過度依賴短期走勢
- 市寬不考慮交易量（但TRIN考慮了）
- 市寬可能無法反映整體市場
- 錯誤信號可能產生
